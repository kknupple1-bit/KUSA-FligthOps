KUSA FlightOps v5.25.37
TOLD Attention-to-Mission Navigation

- TOLD operational-summary attention items are now selectable buttons.
- Selecting an attention item opens the Mission view, scrolls to the relevant review/gate section, highlights it, and focuses the control when applicable.
- Takeoff warning/NO-GO status badge is actionable for obstacle-review and takeoff-performance gates.
- Landing warning/NO-GO status badge is actionable for landing-performance gates.
- Added direct navigation targets for passenger seating, runway selection, NOTAM verification, obstacle review, FOB verification, low-fuel confirmation, takeoff performance, and landing performance.
- Positive PASS/GO status badges remain informational and non-clickable.
- No performance calculation source or numeric data changed in this revision.
