KUSA FlightOps v5.25.5
Pilot Obstacle Review Disposition Priority

FIXED
- Selecting "Pilot reviewed / no conflict identified" now clears the pending obstacle-review gate after takeoff evaluation.
- A prior FAA NMS obstacle NOTAM flag no longer overrides the recorded pilot disposition.
- TOLD now reports the completed review instead of continuing to display AUTO OBSTACLE REVIEW FLAGGED.
- The automated obstacle review card retains the number of flagged obstacle NOTAM items and displays REVIEWED / NO CONFLICT after pilot disposition.
- Selecting "Pilot conflict / do not use runway" remains a blocking NO-GO condition.
- Shared/printed TOLD text reflects the effective pilot review disposition.

STATUS PRIORITY
1. Pilot conflict -> NO-GO / blocking.
2. Flagged or unavailable automated review with no pilot disposition -> pending review.
3. Pilot reviewed / no conflict -> review complete; flagged item count retained as informational.
4. Automated scan with no obstacle NOTAMs -> review complete.

NOTE
FAA NMS obstacle scanning is a NOTAM text review only. It does not evaluate terrain, surveyed obstacles, departure paths, or certified obstacle clearance.
