7. Performance Evaluation

Performance source control: FTA-PA-001019 Rev C is the current controlling Dash-4 AFMS. Section 5 performance pages are unchanged by Rev C and remain active. DTM813/DTM912 remain controlling where not superseded. Digitization audit: balanced-field accelerate-stop is displayed by the AFM definition (ASD = BFL at the selected balanced-field V1). Phase 6 directly digitized the S+Flaps 20° §5.45.6 nomograph panels (pressure-altitude/temperature, weight-family crossings, runway-slope correction, wind correction and field-length axis) into a separate source-controlled shadow dataset. §5.45.6 BFL shadow now passes all four published anchors after the REF-line traversal correction. Phase 11 continues §5.45.7 V1 source control: the runway-slope family remains directly digitized, the weight-family geometry is now directly extracted and registered across the 30,000-lb REF line, and the top PA/temperature solid-family geometry has been extracted for label/applicability trace verification. Headwind/tailwind digitization remains incomplete. The continuous V1 solver remains shadow-only. SHADOW NOMOGRAPH: loading regression… The S+Flaps 20° dry BFL/V1 shadow engine has completed the controlled 12-point software-regression gate; operator/human trace signoff and the anti-ice BFL/V1 family remain pending, so the production grid is not yet promoted or relabeled. Dash-4 takeoff N1 is now formally regression-checked against FTA-PA-001019 Rev I/R for both ice-protection OFF and engine ice-protection ON, including bounded interpolation. Emergency-return VREF is direct DTM813 §5.60.5 data; §5.60.4 landing distance remains pending. No extrapolation. DEVELOPMENT / NOT FOR FLIGHT USE.
